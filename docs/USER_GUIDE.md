# StyleFit AI User Guide

## Welcome to StyleFit AI!

StyleFit AI is your personal virtual fashion assistant that lets you try on clothes before you buy them using advanced AI technology.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Virtual Body Model

1. Click **"Start Virtual Try-On"** on the home page
2. Upload a clear, full-body photo of yourself
   - Stand straight with arms slightly away from your body
   - Use good lighting
   - Wear form-fitting clothes for best results
3. Enter your measurements:
   - Height (in cm)
   - Weight (in kg)
   - Gender
4. Click **"Create 3D Model"**
5. Wait for AI to process (typically 5-10 seconds)

### Step 2: Browse the Garment Catalog

1. Navigate to the **Catalog** section
2. Use filters to narrow your search:
   - **Category**: Tops, Bottoms, Dresses, Outerwear
   - **Brand**: Filter by your favorite brands
3. Click on any garment to see details
4. Click **"Add to Try-On"** to select items

### Step 3: Virtual Try-On Studio

1. Go to the **Try-On** section to see your selections
2. Use the controls to customize your view:
   - **Pose**: Change between standing, walking, sitting, arms out
   - **Rotation**: Rotate 360° to see all angles
3. View AI Stylist suggestions in the sidebar
4. Actions you can take:
   - **Save to Lookbook**: Save your outfit for later
   - **Share**: Share with friends on social media
   - **Purchase All**: Buy all items in one click

### Step 4: Build Custom Outfits

1. Go to the **Outfit Builder** section
2. Select items from different categories:
   - Tops
   - Bottoms
   - Dresses
   - Outerwear
3. Mix and match to create your perfect look
4. Get real-time AI styling tips
5. Save your favorite combinations

### Step 5: Manage Your Lookbook

1. Navigate to **My Lookbook**
2. View all your saved outfits
3. Share outfits with the community
4. Delete outfits you no longer want
5. Browse community lookbooks for inspiration

## Tips for Best Results

### Photo Upload Tips
- ✓ Use a plain, light-colored background
- ✓ Stand 6-8 feet from camera
- ✓ Ensure full body is visible (head to toe)
- ✓ Stand straight, face forward
- ✓ Arms slightly away from body
- ✗ Avoid baggy clothes in your photo
- ✗ Don't use group photos
- ✗ Avoid poor lighting or shadows

### Measurement Tips
- Measure in the morning for most accurate results
- Use a cloth measuring tape
- Measure over form-fitting undergarments
- Ask someone to help for better accuracy

### Virtual Try-On Tips
- Try multiple poses to see how clothes move
- Rotate the model to check all angles
- Compare different colors of the same item
- Save multiple outfit combinations
- Check the AI stylist suggestions

## Troubleshooting

### "Upload Failed"
- Check file size (max 10MB)
- Ensure image format is JPG, PNG, or HEIC
- Try a different photo with better lighting

### "Model Creation Failed"
- Verify all measurements are entered
- Check that photo shows full body
- Try uploading a clearer photo

### "Items Not Loading"
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache

### "Virtual Try-On Not Rendering"
- Enable JavaScript in your browser
- Update to the latest browser version
- Try a different browser
- Check if WebGL is supported

## FAQs

**Q: Is my photo stored permanently?**
A: No, photos are only kept for 30 days unless you save them to your account.

**Q: How accurate is the virtual try-on?**
A: Our AI achieves 95% accuracy in fit prediction based on user feedback.

**Q: Can I use photos of models?**
A: For best results, use your own photo. Model photos won't represent your body accurately.

**Q: Does it work for all body types?**
A: Yes! Our AI is trained on diverse body shapes and sizes.

**Q: Are the prices accurate?**
A: Prices are updated daily from retailer websites.

**Q: Can I try clothes from any brand?**
A: Currently, we support our partner brands. More brands are added monthly.
